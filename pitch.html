The Problem

Ghanaian university students face significant barriers to academic self-management.

quiz

No Accurate Tracking

Students lack reliable tools to calculate and monitor their cumulative GPA across semesters.

wifi_off

Unreliable Internet

Campus connectivity is inconsistent, making cloud-dependent solutions impractical for everyday use.

psychology_alt

No Predictive Guidance

Students cannot forecast how current performance will affect their final GPA or graduation standing.

event_busy

No Scenario Planning

There is no easy way to simulate "what-if" grade scenarios to plan study priorities effectively.

warning

The Result?

Students are left guessing their academic standing, unable to make informed decisions about course loads, retakes, or graduation timelines — leading to preventable academic setbacks and stress.

Our Solution

AZ GPA Calculator is an all-in-one academic intelligence tool built specifically for the Ghanaian student context.

calculate

Accurate GPA Engine

Precise semester and cumulative GPA calculation following Ghanaian university grading standards.

trending_up

GPA Forecasting

Predict final GPA outcomes based on current performance trajectories across all enrolled courses.

storage

Persistent Storage

Save and access semester-by-semester records locally — no account required, no data loss.

science

What-If Simulation

Simulate grade scenarios to understand the impact of different outcomes before exams.

smart_toy

AI Advisory

Personalised AI-powered academic advice and optimisation suggestions based on student data.

wifi_off

Offline-First Design

Fully functional without internet — built from the ground up for Africa's connectivity reality.

"One app that gives every Ghanaian university student the academic clarity and confidence they deserve — regardless of connectivity."
