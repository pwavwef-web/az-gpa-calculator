Slide 02  ·  The Problem

Academic Uncertainty is Real

Ghanaian university students—particularly at UCC and other institutions—face daily challenges that directly impact their academic performance and mental well-being.

calculate

No Reliable GPA Tool

Students rely on mental math or generic online calculators that don't align with their specific grading systems.

wifi_off

Unreliable Internet

Campus connectivity is inconsistent, making cloud-dependent apps unreliable for everyday academic use.

trending_down

No Predictive Guidance

There are no tools to help students forecast what GPA they can achieve based on remaining coursework and targets.

psychology_alt

Academic Anxiety

The absence of data-driven planning tools creates unnecessary stress and poor academic decision-making.

" Students lack a single, offline-capable, locally-aware academic intelligence tool to plan and track their GPA journey. "

Slide 03  ·  The Solution

Introducing AZ GPA Calculator

An intelligent, offline-first Flutter mobile app purpose-built for Ghanaian university students — combining precise GPA calculation with AI-powered academic forecasting.

wifi_off

Works Offline

Full functionality without any internet connection — designed for campus realities.

trending_up

GPA Forecasting

Predictive engine calculates projected GPA based on current performance and future courses.

psychology

AI Advisory

Personalised academic guidance to help students make smarter study decisions.

Core Capabilities

  • ✓ Accurate semester-by-semester GPA calculation aligned to Ghanaian grading systems
  • ✓ Long-term persistent storage of all academic records locally on device
  • ✓ "What-if" scenario simulation — model different grade outcomes before they happen
  • ✓ AI-generated personalised academic improvement recommendations
  • ✓ Clean, student-friendly interface optimised for mobile performance
