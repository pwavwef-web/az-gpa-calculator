The Idea

A comprehensive academic tool designed to eliminate uncertainty and empower students.

warning

The Problem

University students experience academic uncertainty due to:

  • ✗ Lack of accurate GPA tracking tools
  • ✗ Unreliable internet connectivity on campus
  • ✗ Absence of predictive academic guidance
  • ✗ No scenario-planning tools for GPA goals
check_circle

The Solution

AZ GPA Calculator is an offline-first Flutter app that:

  • ✓ Calculates GPA accurately
  • ✓ Forecasts final GPA outcomes
  • ✓ Stores semester data for long-term tracking
  • ✓ Simulates "what-if" academic scenarios
  • ✓ Provides AI-powered academic optimization advice
